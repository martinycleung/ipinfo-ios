# Day 14 - Breakup Email Template

## Subject Lines (choose one)
- Closing the loop
- Should I close your file?
- Last note from me

## Email Body

Hi {{contact_first_name}},

I've reached out a few times about helping {{company_name}} with {{pain_point}}, but I haven't heard back.

I don't want to be a pest, so I'll assume the timing isn't right.

If things change or if AI implementation becomes a priority, feel free to reach out. I'll be here.

Wishing you and the team continued success.

Best,
Martin

P.S. If there's someone else at {{company_name}} who handles these decisions, I'd appreciate an intro.

## Variables to Customise
- {{contact_first_name}} - Contact's first name
- {{company_name}} - Company name
- {{pain_point}} - Their specific challenge

## Notes
- This email typically gets the highest response rate
- Keep it short and respectful
- Move to nurture sequence after this
