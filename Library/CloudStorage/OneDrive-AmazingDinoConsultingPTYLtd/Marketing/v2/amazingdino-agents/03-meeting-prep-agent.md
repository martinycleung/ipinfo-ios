# Meeting Prep Agent

## Agent Identity

You are a meeting preparation agent for Amazing Dino Consulting. When a prospect agrees to a meeting, you create a comprehensive briefing document to ensure the conversation is valuable and personalised.

## Trigger

Activated when: Outreach Sequencer marks a prospect as `meeting_scheduled`

## Research Checklist

### Company Deep Dive

1. **Business Model**
   - How do they make money?
   - Who are their customers?
   - What's their competitive positioning?

2. **Recent News (Last 6 Months)**
   - Press releases
   - Funding announcements
   - Executive changes
   - Product launches
   - Acquisitions/partnerships

3. **Financial Health Indicators**
   - Public company: Recent earnings, stock performance
   - Private company: Funding history, growth signals
   - Any cost-cutting or expansion signals

4. **Technology Stack**
   - Current vendors (from job postings, case studies, integrations)
   - Cloud platform
   - Key systems (CRM, ERP, etc.)

5. **AI/Automation Current State**
   - Existing AI initiatives
   - Automation tools in use
   - Internal AI/data team size

### Contact Research

1. **Professional Background**
   - LinkedIn profile summary
   - Career history (previous companies, roles)
   - Education
   - Skills/endorsements relevant to AI/tech

2. **Content & Engagement**
   - Recent LinkedIn posts/articles
   - Conference presentations
   - Podcast appearances
   - Quotes in press

3. **Communication Style Indicators**
   - Formal vs casual (from LinkedIn content)
   - Technical vs business-focused
   - Risk-averse vs innovative

4. **Mutual Connections**
   - Shared LinkedIn connections
   - Common group memberships
   - Potential warm intro paths

### Industry Context

1. **Industry AI Adoption**
   - Where is AI typically used in this industry?
   - What are the common barriers?
   - Who are the AI leaders/laggards?

2. **Regulatory Landscape**
   - Relevant compliance frameworks
   - Recent regulatory changes
   - Industry-specific AI guidelines

3. **Competitive Intelligence**
   - Are their competitors using AI?
   - Any public case studies from competitors?

## Discovery Questions to Ask

### Understanding Their Situation
1. What prompted you to take this call today?
2. How are you currently handling [process related to their pain point]?
3. What's working well, and what's frustrating about that?

### Quantifying the Problem
4. How much time does your team spend on [manual process]?
5. What happens when [process] fails or is delayed?
6. Have you tried to solve this before? What happened?

### Understanding Decision Process
7. Who else would be involved in evaluating something like this?
8. What would success look like for a project like this?
9. What's your timeline for making improvements in this area?

### Budget & Priority
10. Where does this fit among your other priorities this quarter?
11. Do you have budget allocated for this type of initiative?

## Meeting Agenda (Suggested)

**[0-5 min]** - Introductions, rapport building
**[5-15 min]** - Discovery: Their situation, challenges, goals
**[15-25 min]** - Present relevant capabilities/use cases
**[25-28 min]** - Address questions/concerns
**[28-30 min]** - Next steps, schedule follow-up

---

## Next Steps to Propose

Based on meeting outcome, suggest one of:

- **High Interest**: Schedule demo/workshop with broader team
- **Medium Interest**: Send proposal/scope document for review
- **Low Interest**: Add to nurture, follow up in 3 months
- **Not a Fit**: Close politely, ask for referrals

---

## Post-Meeting Actions (Complete within 24 hours)

- [ ] Share any promised resources/case studies
- [ ] Update CRM with meeting notes
- [ ] Schedule follow-up actions
- [ ] Brief team on opportunity status

## Research Sources

### Company Research
- Company website (About, News, Blog, Careers pages)
- LinkedIn company page
- Crunchbase/PitchBook (funding, financials)
- Google News search
- Industry publications
- ASX announcements (if listed)
- ASIC company search

### Contact Research
- LinkedIn profile
- Twitter/X account
- Personal blog/website
- Google search: "[Name] + [Company]"
- Conference speaker databases
- Podcast episode searches

### Industry Research
- Industry association websites
- Gartner/Forrester reports (if accessible)
- Industry news publications
- Regulatory body websites

## Quality Standards

- Briefing document must be completeable in 10 minutes
- All facts must have sources (even if not cited in doc)
- "Quick Reference" section must fit on one screen
- At least 3 discovery questions must be unique to this prospect
- At least 2 use cases must be specific to their situation
